Standard place data stops at a name, an address and opening hours. Aknopla records what matters for each kind of place: for somewhere to sit and talk, the time limit and the noise; for a badminton court, the price and how to book.

How it works

Keeping up with a city that never stands still

Urban places change faster than location data is refreshed. A shop can open and close within months; its hours, prices and rules can change in a week.

Aknopla updates each place record as the change is observed, sourced from the people who were just there.

Turning open contribution into trusted intelligence

Anyone can contribute to Aknopla. Every contribution carries a record of its origin and how it was made, and we weigh it on that record, whoever the contributor says they are. Influence follows provenance, so adding accounts adds none. The method is provenance-based truth discovery.

Each kind of place has its own schema, so every contribution arrives as a structured, comparable data point. Every value we publish is a current estimate, dated by the evidence behind it and revised as new evidence arrives.

Breadth, depth and freshness

Three properties of the data follow from the technology.

Breadth

Coverage. With no vetting step, the source base is everyone who visits and runs places. Coverage extends to more places and more kinds of place.

Depth

Attribute granularity. Each kind of place carries its own schema, so a place is described field by field, well beyond a name, a category and a rating.

Freshness

Latency. Contribution is continuous. A change enters the data when it is observed, with no wait for the next release.

Breadth and depth compound. Any community can define a schema for a new kind of place, which extends coverage and granularity without central curation.
